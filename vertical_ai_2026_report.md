# YC P26, S26 & W26 vertical-AI website research

> Scope: Spring 2026 (P26), Summer 2026 (S26), and Winter 2026 (W26). A company is counted as vertical AI when AI/ML/agentic automation/autonomy is central and the core workflow is tailored to a clearly identified industry or profession.

## Method

1. Enumerated all YC companies in the official directory: 194 P26, 231 S26, and 199 W26 (624 total).
2. Inspected all 624 official YC company profiles; 620 exposed an external website link.
3. Applied a conservative screen to all 624 records. The audit uses YC descriptions/tags and, when captured, the live homepage title, visible H1, and first-viewport copy. Horizontal AI infrastructure and generic productivity/sales/marketing tools are not treated as vertical AI unless the target workflow is clearly industry-specific.
4. Captured desktop first-view screenshots (1440 × 1000 px) for candidate sites. A failed/blocked first view is recorded rather than inferred.
5. Scored captured first views from 1–5 for clarity, visual polish, industry specificity, and product proof. A score of 4–5 means “strong design reference” only; it is an editorial screen, not a company/product endorsement.
6. “Hero slogan” is the first visible homepage H1 captured live. When no visible H1 was available, the index explicitly says so rather than substituting a guessed slogan.
7. The vertical-AI classification and visual quality scores are model-assisted screening judgments; use them to prioritize review, and inspect linked live pages before adopting design decisions.

## Strongest website patterns observed

- **Lead with a concrete job or outcome.** The strongest pages tell the buyer what gets faster, safer, cheaper, or more reliable before explaining the AI.
- **Make the vertical obvious.** A hospital, factory, job site, brokerage, grid, or legal workflow visual helps the visitor immediately recognize the buyer and use case.
- **Show product proof early.** Interface mockups, workflow diagrams, service evidence, or realistic operational imagery outperform abstract “AI” claims.
- **Pair specificity with a simple CTA.** Strong pages keep the first action visible and avoid making the visitor decode a broad company mission.
